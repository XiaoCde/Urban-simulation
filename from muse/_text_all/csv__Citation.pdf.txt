Citation 
 
To recognize the valuable role of the NextGen National Household Travel Survey (NHTS) data 
in the transportation research process and to facilitate repeatability of the research, users of 
NHTS data are asked to formally acknowledge the data source. Where possible, this 
acknowledgement should take place in the form of a formal citation, such as when writing a 
research report, planning document, on-line article, and other publications. The citation can be 
formatted as follows: 
Federal Highway Administration. (2022). 2022 NextGen National Household Travel Survey Core 
Data, 
U.S. 
Department 
of 
Transportation, 
Washington, 
DC. 
Available 
online: 
http://nhts.ornl.gov.
