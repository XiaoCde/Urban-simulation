# 条9 · DC 信号等待时长与城市道路车速分布

（第六轮回件，2026-11-16。内容为检索原件的逐字抄录与出处标注；标「没有」者附检索记录。）

## 条9 · 华盛顿特区：(a) 信号等待时长分布；(b) 城市道路车速分布

### 9(a) 信号周期/红灯时长

**结论：信号等待时长分布、红灯时长分布：没有**（DDOT 未发布全市信号周期长或红灯时长的分布统计）。最接近的相关内容如下。

**(1) DDOT 信号系统白皮书 [全文（在线阅读；PDF 直接下载失败，服务器对直链下载返回 HTML，未存入 files-E）]**
- 文献：District Department of Transportation, Transportation Operations Administration, Traffic Signals, Traffic Safety, Standards and ITS Division, "The District of Columbia Traffic Signal System: 1971 to 2011", rev. 3.3.11（2011）。原件链接：http://d92016.eos-intl.net/eLibSQL14_D92016_Documents/The%20District%20of%20Columbia%20Traffic%20Signal%20System%20-%201971%20to%202011.pdf
- 原文逐字：
  - "The District's central traffic signal and CCTV command and control system, QuicNet, monitors over 1500 traffic signals and devices within the District."
  - "primarily utilizing four (4) timing plans based on the AM Peak, Mid-Day, PM Peak, and Nights and Weekend traffic. The system also maintains one "240 second" cycle plan for Emergency Evacuation along regionally based published evacuation routes."
  - "the District has maintained short cycle lengths (100 second plan during the peak hours)"；并述低周期长旨在使行人与机动车等待时间与排队保持在合理水平。
  - "With the exception of a few actuated intersections, the majority of the signals in the District operate under pre-timed control."
- 口径：全市信号系统，2011年口径；上述为单一周期长数值（高峰100 s、疏散预案240 s），非分布。

**(2) DDOT FY2015 绩效报告 [索引]**
- 文献：District Department of Transportation, FY 2015 Performance Accountability Report（2016年1月发布）。https://doc.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/oca/publication/attachments/DDOT_FY15PAR.pdf
- 数字：全市信号化路口 "currently 1680"；Phase Two 优化约635个信号；优化后全网行程时间节省乘用车15.6%、公交5%（Phase Two 范围为 downtown 区域：Rock Creek Parkway 以东、North Capitol 以西、U Street/Florida Avenue 以南、I-395 以北）。

**(3) ITE 奖项条目（TRID）[索引]**
- 文献： "ITE Transportation Achievement Awards: DDOT Citywide Signal Optimization Program", *ITE Journal*, 2024-04（TRID Accession 01950196）。https://trid.trb.org/View/2518166
- 数字：该项目对全市 "all 1,700+ traffic signals" 做持续轮换的信号配时优化。

### 9(b) 城市道路车速分布

**结论：DC 城市道路（干道/街道）的车速分布（分档/分位数）：没有**（DDOT 公开渠道未发布此类分布；DDOT 白皮书载其车辆检测系统在122处地点采集 volume、speed、occupancy 数据，但未公布分布）。最接近的相关内容如下。

**(1) INRIX Global Traffic Scorecard 的 DC downtown 速度（单点值，非分布）[索引]**
- 来源：INRIX, *2025 Global Traffic Scorecard*（分析师 Bob Pishue, 2025年12月；数据至2025年上半年）演示稿 PDF：https://storage.pardot.com/171932/1764190109BRPoNcYU/INRIX_2025_Global_Traffic_Scorecard.pdf ；及历年新闻稿转引。
- 数字（Washington DC urban area）：2025年 delay 70 小时/驾驶人（2024年62小时，+13%），downtown speed **11 mph**，delay cost $1,289/驾驶人；2023年版：delay 63 小时，average downtown speed **11 mph**；2022年版：delay 83 小时，downtown speed **11 mph**。
- 口径（INRIX 定义，转引自 Scorecard 说明）："Downtown speed is the speed at which a commuter should expect to travel 1 mile into the city's downtown or central business area during peak morning hours"；数据为匿名 GPS 探针数据；地理口径为都会区（urban area），非 DC 行政边界内的街道网络。

**(2) TPB 2011 空中交通拥堵调查的高速路段速度 [索引]**
- 文献：National Capital Region Transportation Planning Board (TPB) / MWCOG, 2011 Aerial Traffic Congestion Survey（第七版，自1993年起每三年一次；仅覆盖区域高速公路，早晚高峰）。新闻稿（2011-12-02）：https://www.mwcog.org/about-us/newsroom/2011/12/02/results-from-regional-traffic-congestion-analysis-released-bottlenecks-congestion-highways-and-roads-traffic-monitoring-travel-patterns/
- 最拥堵地点清单原文（节选）：
  - "Northbound I-395 (from VA 27/Washington Boulevard to VA 110/Jefferson Davis Highway): From 8:30 to 9:30 am, average speed is 5 miles per hour"
  - "Southbound I-395/Southwest Freeway (from 4th Street to 12th Street): From 6:00 to 7:00 pm, average speed is 5-10 miles per hour"（此段位于 DC 境内）
  - "Inner Loop I-495 (from VA 193/Georgetown Pike to George Washington Memorial Parkway): From 5:30 to 6:30 pm, average speed is 5-10 miles per hour"
  - "Eastbound I-66 (from VA 7/Leesburg Pike to Dulles Access Road): From 6:00 to 7:00 pm, average speed is 7-10 miles per hour"
  - I-495 两段（MD 355/I-270 至 MD 185；VA 267 至 VA 123）：晚高峰平均速度 10-15 mph。
- 口径：高速公路瓶颈段、特定1小时时段的平均速度；非城市街道、非分布。

**(3) DDOT 检测系统（数据采集存在、分布未公布）[全文]**
- 同 9(a)(1) 白皮书： "Vehicle Detection System — Project Limit: 122 locations throughout the District"，采集 "traffic volume, speed, and occupancy and vehicle classification data"（2010-10-07 NTP，计划2011-10-07完成）；白皮书未给出任何速度统计结果。
