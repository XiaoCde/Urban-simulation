# 条5 · 层高实测与规范值 + DC 首层抬高（freeboard）条文

（第六轮回件，2026-11-16。内容为检索原件的逐字抄录与出处标注；标「没有」者附检索记录。）

## 条5(a) 美国住宅/办公 floor-to-floor 层高：实测统计与规范值

**结论：实测统计找到两项 [全文]；规范方面，IBC/IRC 只规定净高（ceiling height）下限，未找到任何 floor-to-floor 层高的规范值——该项没有；RSMeans 的公开可检索原文数值没有。**

**（1）Choi (2002) 高层建筑统计研究 [全文]**
- 作者/出处：Yong Sun Choi, "The Preliminary Design Guideline for Tall Building: Exploration of Planning Factors & Building Factors", *Architectural Research* Vol. 4 No. 1 (2002), pp. 1–6（KoreaScience 平台 PDF，原件已存档：`Choi2002_Tall_Building_Design_Guideline.pdf`；官方链接：https://www.koreascience.kr/article/JAKO200220852873795.pdf ）。
- 样本原文："data of 130 tall buildings, based on Chicago area and some of the significant building in the around world"（数据来源为 CTBUH 资料与芝加哥地区调查；样本为高层建筑，非一般建筑存量）。
- 原文："Based on data, see figure-12, the most common floor-to-floor height for **office** is range from **3.5 to 4.1M (11ft 6in to 13ft 4in)** and the floor-to-floor height for **residential and hotel** function are ranges from **2.6 to 2.9M (8ft 6in to 9ft 6in)**. Consequently, **the average for floor to floor height should be 3.8M (12ft 5in) for office and 2.7M (9ft) for residential & hotel**."

**（2）Jedwab, Barr & Brueckner (2022) CTBUH 数据回归 [全文]**
- 出处：Remi Jedwab, Jason Barr, Jan K. Brueckner, "Cities Without Skylines: Worldwide Building-Height Gaps and their Possible Determinants and Implications", *Journal of Urban Economics*, Vol. 132 (2022)，DOI 10.1016/j.jue.2022.103507；其 Web Appendix A "Details on the Building Heights Data"（Elsevier 附件 PDF，原件已存档：`JUE_WebAppendix_Building_Heights_Data.pdf`；链接：https://ars.els-cdn.com/content/image/1-s2.0-S0094119022000833-mmc1.pdf ）。
- 数据原文："The original data set of CTBUH (2018) … has 27,652 tall buildings. Once we keep 'buildings' and 'tower-buildings' that are completed or about to be completed, we are left with 19,132 tall buildings."
- 原文："We then regress our measure of height on the number of floors and find a **coefficient of 3.8\*\*\***, which indicates that a floor corresponds to 4 meters for most buildings in the world (**95% conf. interval = [3.77; 3.87]**)."
- 口径注：该附录自述其数据集主要覆盖 80 m 以上建筑，对 80 m 以下建筑 "is likely unreliable"；上引回归段落未按住宅/办公分列（数据集中已知功能建筑里住宅占 49.9%、办公占 41.8%）。

**（3）IBC / IRC 条文（净高，非 floor-to-floor）[全文（条文经州规范文件与规范镜像逐字核对）]**
- IBC 2021 §1208.2 (Minimum ceiling heights) 原文："**Occupiable spaces, habitable spaces and corridors shall have a ceiling height of not less than 7 feet 6 inches (2286 mm) above the finished floor. Bathrooms, toilet rooms, kitchens, storage rooms and laundry rooms shall have a ceiling height of not less than 7 feet (2134 mm) above the finished floor.**"（IBC 2024 同条号同数值。）
- IRC 2021 §R305.1 (Minimum height) 原文："**Habitable space, hallways and portions of basements containing these spaces shall have a ceiling height of not less than 7 feet (2134 mm). Bathrooms, toilet rooms and laundry rooms shall have a ceiling height of not less than 6 feet 8 inches (2032 mm).**"（2024 IRC 改编号为 §R313.1，数值相同。）
- 事实说明：以上条文规定的是 ceiling height（净高）下限；本轮在 IBC/IRC 中**没有**找到设定 floor-to-floor 层高的条文。

**（4）RSMeans：没有。**
- 检索来源：公开网络检索（关键词 "RSMeans" "floor-to-floor height" / "ceiling height" 默认值与典型值）。RSMeans 的相关数值位于其付费造价数据（纸质/电子版）内，未取得可逐字引用的公开原文数值。
- 对照（按任务说明，不重复检索）：PNNL 商业建筑 floor-to-floor 3.95 m 为已知数，仅作对照。

## 条5(b) DC 首层抬高（freeboard）：DC 条文与 FEMA NFIP 口径

**结论：找到。DC 现行条文 [全文]；FEMA NFIP 联邦最低要求与 freeboard 定义 [全文/索引]。**

### （1）DC 现行条文：20 DCMR Chapter 31 (Flood Hazard Rules)，2025 年最终规则

- 发布机构：District of Columbia Department of Energy and Environment（DOEE）。
- 文件全名：Notice of Final Rulemaking — Flood Hazard Rules，修订 Title 20 (Environment), DCMR Chapter 31 (Flood Hazard Rules)。提案于 2024-07-19 刊于 DC Register（71 DCR 8695）；最终规则 2025-06-10 通过，**2025-06-20 刊于 DC Register 起生效**。
- 官方链接：https://www.ddoe.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/doee/publication/attachments/DOEE%20Flood%20Hazard%20Rules%2020%20DCMR%20Ch31%20Final%20June%2020%202025.pdf （原件已存档：`DC_DOEE_Flood_Hazard_Rules_20DCMR31_Final_2025-06-20.pdf`）
- 适用范围原文（§3101.2，洪泛危害区定义）："The flood hazard area is: (a) The land inside the one hundred (100)-year floodplain (Zones A, AE, A1-30, A99, AR, AO, or AH) in accordance with the FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Map, effective September 27, 2010, and subsequent revisions and amendments as approved by FEMA; (b) The land inside the five hundred (500)-year floodplain (Zone X (shaded)) in accordance with the FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Map, effective September 27, 2010, and subsequent revisions and amendments as approved by FEMA; (c) The land that has been removed from the five hundred (500)-year floodplain …"（(d) 为 Rhode Island Avenue Metro underpass 区域，见 §3107.8。）即 2025 年规则把受规制区域由 FEMA 100 年洪泛区扩展至 500 年洪泛区，适用于区内的新建与实质性改造（substantial improvement），豁免情形除外。

- **设计洪水高程 DFE（§3107.3）原文**："Unless otherwise designated, the DFE shall be the higher of: (a) **The base flood elevation plus a freeboard equal to two feet (2 ft.)**, or (b) **The high flood elevation**, in accordance with the most current FIS and FIRM published by FEMA and adopted by the District."
  ——即一般建筑：DFE = BFE + 2 ft freeboard 与 500 年洪水高程（high flood elevation）二者取高。
- **关键设施（§3107.4）**：DFE 为 §3107.3 所定值**再加 two feet (2 ft.)**（原文："the DFE shall be equivalent to that identified in § 3107.3 **plus two feet (2 ft.)**."）。
- **潮汐岸线缓冲区新建/实质改造（§3107.5）**：DFE 为 §3107.3 所定值**再加 three and four-tenths feet (3.4 ft.)**。
- **Zone X (shaded) 中经 LOMR 15-03-2388P 划定且 FIS/FIRM 无 high flood elevation 的区域（§3107.7）原文要点**："the DFE shall be the elevation of the base flood elevation plus freeboard of two feet (2 ft.)…"。
- **Rhode Island Avenue Metro underpass 区域（§3107.8）**：DFE = **96.5 ft NAVD88**。
- **住宅结构最低楼层（§3109.1, Residential Structures — Lowest Floor Height）原文**："For the new construction or substantial improvement of residential structures wholly or partially inside the flood hazard areas specified in § 3101.2(a), the lowest floor (including the basement, parking garage, and any ancillary residential uses) **shall be at least the height of the DFE**…"（§3101.2(b)–(d) 区域内的车库及附属住宅用途部分另有 dry floodproofing 替代路径。）
- **非住宅结构（§3110.1）原文要点**：lowest floor (including basement) "either shall be at least the height of the DFE or the entire structure shall be designed and constructed to be **dry floodproofed** during any flood up to the DFE height."
- **混合用途（§3111.1–3111.4）原文要点**：最低楼层须 ≥ DFE 或干式防洪至 DFE（§3111.1）；住宅部分须 ≥ DFE（§3111.2）；非住宅部分须 ≥ DFE 或干式防洪至 DFE（§3111.3）；附属住宅部分须 ≥ DFE（§3111.4）。
- **定义（第 31 章 §3199 定义部分）原文**：
  - "Design Flood Elevation (DFE) – the one hundred (100)-year flood elevation (base flood elevation) plus two feet (2 ft.), or the five hundred (500)-year flood elevation (high flood elevation), whichever is higher, based on the FEMA Flood Insurance Rate Map, effective September 27, 2010, and subsequent revisions and amendments as approved by FEMA…"
  - "Freeboard – a factor of safety usually expressed in feet above a flood level for purposes of floodplain management intended to compensate for the many unknown factors that could contribute to flood heights greater than the height calculated for a selected size flood and floodway conditions, such as wave action, bridge openings, and the hydrological effect of urbanization of the watershed."
- DOEE 在最终规则前言中对 freeboard 取向的原文表述："DOEE uses a freeboard value above the Base Flood Elevation…"，并称所采标准为 "a 100-year flood elevation plus 2 feet of freeboard standard"。

### （2）DC 旧规（已被 2025 年规则取代）[索引，经 DOEE 自身备忘录逐字转引]

- 旧 20 DCMR §3105.2 原文（DOEE 2017-07-07 备忘录（Z.C. Case 17-05）及 2017-05-26 DOEE 报告转引）："Within SFHAs, the lowest floor (including basement) of any new construction of, or substantial improvement to, residential structures **shall be at least one and one-half feet (1-1/2 ft.) above the base flood elevation** and shall be verified by an Elevation Certificate (FEMA Form 81-31)"
- 旧 §3105.3：非住宅同为 BFE 以上 1.5 ft，或防洪至该高度，并以 Elevation Certificate (FEMA Form 81-31) 与 Floodproofing Certificate (FEMA 81-65) 验证。
- 注：2025 年新章中 §3105 已改为费用（Fees）条，条号体系已变更。

### （3）FEMA NFIP 联邦口径

- **freeboard 定义（44 CFR §59.1）[全文，eCFR 原文]**："**Freeboard** means a factor of safety usually expressed in feet above a flood level for purposes of flood plain management. 'Freeboard' tends to compensate for the many unknown factors that could contribute to flood heights greater than the height calculated for a selected size flood and floodway conditions, such as wave action, bridge openings, and the hydrological effect of urbanization of the watershed."
- 同条其他定义原文："Base flood means the flood having a one percent chance of being equalled or exceeded in any given year"；"Lowest Floor means the lowest floor of the lowest enclosed area (including basement)…"。
- **住宅最低要求（44 CFR §60.3(c)(2)）[索引，经 FindLaw 所载 CFR 文本]** 原文："Require that all new construction and substantial improvements of **residential** structures within Zones A1–30, AE and AH zones on the community's FIRM **have the lowest floor (including basement) elevated to or above the base flood level**, unless the community is granted an exception by the Federal Insurance Administrator for the allowance of basements in accordance with § 60.6(b) or (c)"
- **非住宅最低要求（44 CFR §60.3(c)(3)）[索引]** 原文要点：lowest floor "(i) elevated to or above the base flood level or, (ii) together with attendant utility and sanitary facilities, be designed so that below the base flood level the structure is watertight with walls substantially impermeable to the passage of water and with structural components having the capability of resisting hydrostatic and hydrodynamic loads and effects of buoyancy"。
- **AO 区（44 CFR §60.3(c)(7)）[索引]**：lowest floor 须高于最高相邻地面至少 FIRM 所标深度数；未标深度数时至少 two feet。
- 口径事实：NFIP 联邦最低标准只要求抬高**至** BFE，未规定 freeboard 数值；freeboard 为 BFE 之上的附加高度（见 §59.1 定义）。DC 的 +2 ft（DFE 口径）为地方更高标准。
