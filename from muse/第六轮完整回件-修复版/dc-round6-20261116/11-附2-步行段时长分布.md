## 附2 · 行人连续步行段落（walking bout）的实测时长分布

**结论：找到**（Orendurff et al. 2008 给出完整的 bout 时长/步数/休息分布，见附2-1）。以 NHANES 为样本的 walking-bout **时长分布**：**没有找到**——NHANES 2005–2006 的公开步行分析为 cadence 分档累计时间（附2-2），不是 bout 时长分布。

### 附2-1 Orendurff et al. 2008 [摘要（PubMed）；bout 定义据 Palmer et al. 2014 [全文]]

- 文献：Orendurff, M. S., Schoen, J. A., Bernatz, G. C., Segal, A. D., Klute, G. K., "How humans walk: bout duration, steps per bout, and rest duration", *Journal of Rehabilitation Research and Development*, 45(7): 1077–1089, 2008。DOI 10.1682/jrrd.2007.11.0197；PMID 19165696。https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19165696/
- 样本与设备：10名无残疾在职成人（年龄36.6±14.8岁，该数字据 Palmer et al. 2014 转引）；StepWatch Activity Monitor（OrthoCare Innovations），以10秒为窗口计数步数，连续监测2周。
- 摘要原文逐字： "Sixty percent of all walking bouts lasted just 30 seconds or less; a 2-minute walking bout was just 1 percent of total walking bouts. Forty percent of all walking bouts were less than 12 steps in a row, and 75 percent of all walking bouts were less than 40 steps in a row. Rest periods were predominantly very short, with half of all rests periods lasting 20 seconds or less."
- 二手转引补充 [索引]：后续引用文献转引该研究称 81% 的 walking bouts 短于1分钟（经 ResearchGate 图注页转引，未核原始 Table 2）。
- **Bout 定义口径**（Palmer, K., Thomas, J., Mudge, S., "Activity patterns in people with neurological conditions", *New Zealand Journal of Physiotherapy*, 2014；PDF 已存 files-E/palmer2014_neurological_activity_patterns_orendurff.pdf）原文： "A walking bout was defined as a period of time in which sequential steps occurred in subsequent ten-second intervals, consistent with Cavanaugh et al (2007). A walking bout ended when no steps were recorded in the subsequent ten-second interval." 休息期定义为前后10秒窗口均无步数的时段。
- 同文转引的 Orendurff 健康成人数字（与摘要一致）：60% 的 bouts ≤30 s；≥2 min 的 bouts 仅1%；40% 的 bouts 少于12步；75% 少于40步；休息期50% ≤20 s。

### 附2-2 Tudor-Locke et al. 2011（NHANES cadence 分档时间，非 bout 分布）[摘要]

- 文献：Tudor-Locke, C., Camhi, S. M., Leonardi, C., Johnson, W. D., Katzmarzyk, P. T., Earnest, C. P., Church, T. S., "Patterns of adult stepping cadence in the 2005–2006 NHANES", *Preventive Medicine*, 53(3): 178–181, 2011。
- 样本与设备：3,740名≥20岁成人；ActiGraph AM-7164 加速度计，逐分钟步数；至少1个有效日（佩戴≥10/24 h）。
- 各 cadence 档的日累计时间（摘要原文，经 Tudor-Locke & Rowe 2012, *Sports Medicine* 42(5) 综述摘要复述一致）：0 cadence（非移动）4.8 h/day；1–59 steps/min 约8.7 h/day；60–79 steps/min 约16 min/day；80–99 steps/min 约8 min/day；100–119 steps/min 约5 min/day；120+ steps/min 约2 min/day。
- 口径说明：以上为「处于某 cadence 档的分钟数」，不是连续步行 bout 的时长分布；该研究未给出 bout 中位数/分位数。
