## 1. Thieken et al. 2023（NHESS 23:973–990）— 索取 cut points 完整输出 + 微观数据

- 收件人：Annegret H. Thieken <thieken@uni-potsdam.de>
- 依据：正文 Data availability 原文 "The survey data are owned by the authors and can currently be provided upon reasonable request only."
- 已知结论：正文 XML、PDF、补充材料、预印本均已通读，S2/S3 只印斜率系数，无任何 cut points；公开渠道不存在完整输出。

Subject: Request for ordered-logit cut points (Suppl. Tables S2/S3) and survey microdata — NHESS 23:973–990 (2023)

Dear Professor Thieken,

I am building an agent-based simulation of pedestrian behaviour during urban pluvial flooding in Washington, DC, and your 2021-flood warning-system study (NHESS 23, 973–990, 2023) is the empirical basis for my agents' protective-action model.

The published supplement (Tables S2 and S3) reports the ordered logistic regressions without the threshold (cut-point) estimates, which I need to convert the coefficients into action probabilities. Could you share the complete model output for Tables S2/S3 including the cut points (e.g. the original Stata log or a spreadsheet)?

In addition, the article states that the survey data can be provided upon reasonable request. If possible, I would be grateful for the anonymised individual-level data (CSV or Stata format) together with the codebook. The data would be used solely for non-commercial academic modelling, cited as you specify, and not redistributed.

Thank you very much for your time.
